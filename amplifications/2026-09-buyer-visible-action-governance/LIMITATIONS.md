# Buyer-Visible Action Governance — Limitations and Non-Claims

This amplification is deliberately bounded to the later experimental scenario and evidence identified in `EVIDENCE.md`.

It does **not** establish that:

- the entire ABE architecture is implemented;
- ABE is production ready;
- ABE guarantees correct decisions;
- ABE prevents AI errors;
- ABE governs arbitrary AI systems;
- companies or buyers already understand, validate or prefer ABE;
- ABE has demonstrated market demand, willingness to pay or product-market fit;
- ABE is superior to conventional rule engines, workflow systems, AI systems or human decision processes;
- the recorded 25-test result proves generalized or universal ABE effectiveness;
- ABE is first, unique or historically unprecedented;
- the MVP is registered or registrable at INPI.

The experimental MVP supports only bounded statements about the tested implementation and declared scenarios.

The phrase “ABE governs AI” should not be used as unqualified shorthand for this proof. Publication 001's narrower distinctions among capability, authority, evidence, assistance, responsibility and governance remain controlling.

A future external Path B interaction, if authorized and deployed, concerns comprehension of selected public distinctions. Comprehension evidence must remain separate from engagement, perceived value, buyer status, purchase intent, market demand and product-market fit.

Negative, partial or inconclusive external results must remain reportable rather than being silently removed or converted into favorable claims.
