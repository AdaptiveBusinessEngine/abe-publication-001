# Publication 001 — Public Claim Matrix

**Release layer:** Publication 001 amplification

This matrix governs material public wording about the Adaptive Business Engine (ABE). The same evidence boundary applies to technical documents, repository pages, portfolio material, presentations, websites, social/press summaries and oral explanations.

## Claim classes

- **AUTHORIZED** — supported for direct use within the stated object/scope.
- **AUTHORIZED WITH QUALIFICATION** — permitted only when the stated limitation or historical condition remains visible.
- **UNSUPPORTED** — not currently supported as established fact.
- **PROHIBITED STRONGER FORMULATION** — wording that materially exceeds the evidence and must not be used as shorthand for a weaker authorized claim.

## Authorized core claims

**AUTHORIZED**

- ABE is a governed organizational architecture / experimental framework for evidence-aware organizational decision support, authority, memory, continuity and human–AI cooperation.
- ABE takes an organization-first architectural position in which organizational meaning, authority, evidence, state and history govern implementation rather than being silently defined by software or AI capability.
- ABE distinguishes capability, permission, authority, responsibility, decision and execution when the distinction is material.
- ABE distinguishes evidence, hypothesis, inference, uncertainty and confirmed fact.
- ABE preserves revision, supersession and prior evidentiary state instead of requiring later conclusions to erase the historical basis of earlier decisions.
- First-exposure failures are intentionally preserved as part of the evidence record.

## Unsupported or prohibited stronger claims

Do not state or imply that:

- ABE is universally validated;
- ABE guarantees correct decisions;
- ABE eliminates errors or hallucinations;
- ABE is generally superior to humans, AI systems or conventional approaches;
- passing the holdouts proves universal generalization;
- ABE universally solves Simpson's paradox or detects it in arbitrary datasets;
- ABE is a complete production-ready commercial software platform;
- ABE prevents fraud or would have prevented a specific scam;
- ABE diagnoses disease, changes treatment or provides clinical authorization;
- Publication 001 already implements the future External Evidence Layer / evidence-orchestration capability;
- ABE currently has an automated/semi-automated architectural dependency-impact detector;
- ABE is historically unique, the first architecture of its kind, or proven unprecedented;
- ABE is patented or legally exclusive over abstract ideas, methods or facts;
- `Adaptive Business Engine` / `ABE` is a registered trademark unless official evidence supports that statement;
- H5 was automatically 8/8;
- H6 was 10/10 on pristine first exposure.

## Future-development wording

Future work may be described only with visibly future-facing wording such as `roadmap`, `future`, `proposed`, `candidate` or equivalent.

Consumer/fraud and clinical/medication scenarios may be described as future validation references, not as proof of current fraud-prevention or clinical capability.

## Short-format rule

Short format does not relax the evidence boundary.

If a qualification cannot fit in a headline, card or caption, use a weaker claim that remains true without the omitted qualification rather than compressing a qualified claim into an unqualified one.
